# Geographic data

Land outlines: Natural Earth, 1:110m land polygons.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

License: Public Domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

`land-points.bin` is derived locally by `scripts/generate-globe.mjs`.
No map tiles, external requests or map service keys are used at runtime.
Location markers are approximate thematic gateways to procedural art, not real scanned sites.
